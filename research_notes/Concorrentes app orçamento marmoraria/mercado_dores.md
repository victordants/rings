# How small Brazilian marmorarias quote, sell and operate, and where it hurts (market and pain points)

> Method note: research done 2026-11-25 with web search only. WebFetch could not reach most hosts from this session (reclameaqui.com.br, maqfort.com.br and stonify.com.br failed DNS: the session's network rules block them). Findings therefore come from search-engine summaries and snippets, not full-page reads. Marked **[UNVERIFIED]** where a claim rests on a single snippet or vendor marketing. I found no YouTube comment threads, Facebook group posts or Instagram posts through search, so that part of the brief is a gap.

## 1. Typical quoting workflow (visit, measurement, Excel or notebook, WhatsApp), time per quote, conversion

### Takeaway
The usual flow has two stages. First an estimate, built from the client's or architect's measurements or a quick visit and sent by WhatsApp. Then, after the client accepts and pays the sinal, a technical measurement (medição técnica / gabarito) taken on site once cabinets and wall finishes are in. The final measurement can change the price. The "hours per quote" and "lost quotes in WhatsApp" claims come only from software vendors. I found no independent data on conversion rates.

### Cited Findings
- Process guides describe a preview estimate followed by an on-site technical visit, which "evita divergências entre o valor estimado e o final" — [chegouaminhavez.com.br](https://chegouaminhavez.com.br/como-funciona-o-processo-de-orcamento-em-uma-marmoraria/)
- The final production measurement is taken by the marmorista on site with the masonry finished, often with a gabarito (template). Client-made measurements serve "só para estimativa" — [Marmorarias Perto](https://marmorariasperto.com.br/blog/como-medir-bancada-orcamento)
- The bancada must be measured over cabinets that are installed and levelled, with the wall covering decided, because tile laid later changes the measurement. The cuba, cooktop and torneira should be on hand to check the template. Delivery comes 7 to 20 days after measurement, and the measurement is often free if the order is closed with that shop — [Mármore Brasil](https://marmorebrasil.com.br/blog/visita-de-medicao-marmoraria/)
- Some shops charge for a technical visit: R$200 to R$500 for simple residential rooms (Goiânia) — [marmorariagoiania.com](https://marmorariagoiania.com/servico/consultoria-e-medicao-tecnica-no-local-visita-tecnica/)
- Crooked walls and out-of-square niches are common and can change the installation — [Mister Marmoraria](https://blog.mistermarmoraria.com.br/pt/marmore-e-granito/marmoraria-no-centro-sp-bancadas-pias-e-revestimentos-sob-medida)
- One shop's terms: the production lead time starts only after the sinal is paid and the final measurement is taken — search summary of marmoraria websites (e.g. [LC Mármores](https://www.lcmarmores.com/), [Styllos](https://www.styllosmarmore.com.br/)) **[UNVERIFIED: exact shop not confirmed]**
- Vendor claims about the "before" state: "planilhas confusas e desatualizadas, orçamentos perdidos no WhatsApp, cálculos no papel com erros e demora de horas por orçamento" — [Marmocalc](https://marmocalc.com.br/) **[vendor marketing]**
- Marmo Smart's FAQ claims marmorarias lose 2 to 4 hours a day to quote rework and looking up information, without citing a source — [Marmo Smart](https://www.marmosmart.com.br/) **[vendor marketing, no source]**
- Marmu promises a quote "em menos de dois minutos" — [Marmu](https://www.marmu.com.br/) **[vendor claim]**
- Adjacent trade (marcenaria): a vendor says marceneiros using spreadsheets report an average of 45 minutes per quote, and a testimonial says over an hour — [WoodOrça+](https://woodorcaplus.com.br/blog/sistema-orcamentos.html) **[vendor marketing]**
- On the client side, slow responses are a common complaint about service businesses ("faz 8 dias que pedi orçamento e sempre a mesma demora tanto no telefone quanto no watzap"). That complaint is about a non-marmoraria company, but it shows the pattern — [Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/bunnitu/demora-doi-a-tres-dias-para-responder-e-nao-mandam-orcamento-um-grande-descaso-faz-8-dias-que-pedi-orcamento-e-sempre-a-mesma-demora-tanto-no-telefone-quanto-no-watzap_K_Vh8hhORPpFSp_w/)
- Generic follow-up advice for quotes that go quiet on WhatsApp: 2 to 3 spaced contacts and a short audio or video explaining the quote — [SocialHub](https://www.socialhub.pro/?p=25858)

### Inferences
- An app needs two quote states. One is a "preliminary estimate" based on approximate measurements. The other is a "final quote / order" after the medição técnica, with a visible price change (a delta) and the client re-approving it. Without this, measurement-driven price changes become disputes (see section 4).
- A measurement checklist (cabinets installed? wall covering laid? cuba, cooktop and torneira models on hand?) maps directly to the documented failure causes.
- Quote speed and follow-up matter (clients shop around), but there is no hard data on win rates.

### Gaps
- No independent data on the time spent per marmoraria quote, the number of quotes per week, or quote-to-order conversion rates. Only vendor claims exist.
- I could not find first-person owner accounts (YouTube comments, Facebook groups) through search.

## 2. Pricing practices and what must appear in a proposal

### Takeaway
Stone is priced per m², based on the slab's usable area, so the loss % is built into the m² price. Edge finishes (reto, boleado, meia-esquadria) and frontão/rodabanca are priced per metro linear. Cutouts (cuba, cooktop, torneira holes) are priced per unit. Installation and freight are often separate lines. A common payment pattern is a 50% sinal before production and 50% at installation, with a PIX discount for cash and card instalments. Under CDC art. 40 a service quote must list labour, materials, payment terms and start and finish dates. The quote is valid for 10 days unless it states otherwise, and binds both parties once approved.

### Cited Findings
- Edge finishes are quoted per metro linear and cuba and cooktop cutouts per unit. A typical price table lists "Acabamento reto: R$/metro linear, Boleado: R$/metro linear, Meia-esquadria: R$/metro linear", plus cutouts and holes per unit — search summary of [Maqfort guide](https://www.maqfort.com.br/como-fazer-orcamento-de-marmoraria/) and [Braiker blog](https://blog.braiker.com.br/metro-do-marmore/) **[snippet-level]**
- A simple boleado is cheap, while meia-cana dupla, bisotê and worked edges are charged per metro linear — [Braiker](https://blog.braiker.com.br/metro-do-marmore/) / [Marmorarias Perto](https://marmorariasperto.com.br/blog/valor-do-marmore-o-que-define-preco) **[snippet-level, attribution between the two uncertain]**
- The resin and catalyst used per metre of meia-esquadria is a cost that often "some da conta e come sua margem" — search summary (Maqfort / Herogran blog) **[snippet-level]**
- Loss: "nenhuma operação tem 100% de aproveitamento da chapa", so a shop should work with an average loss %. Worked example: a 3.20 × 1.90 m slab (6.08 m²) with 25% loss leaves 4.56 m² usable. The material cost per m² sold is the slab price ÷ 4.56, not ÷ 6.08 — [Herogran blog: Margem de lucro](https://herograncrm.com.br/blog/margem-de-lucro-da-marmoraria) / [Maqfort](https://www.maqfort.com.br/como-fazer-orcamento-de-marmoraria/)
- An older (2010) post recommends never working with less than 20% loss — [webmarmores](https://webmarmores.wordpress.com/2010/05/31/calculo-de-perda-na-chapa-de-granito/) **[old, rule of thumb]**
- A full m² price build-up: usable-area material cost + share of fixed costs + taxes + commission + card fee + margin. The example came to R$681.87/m² before margin — [Herogran blog](https://herograncrm.com.br/blog/margem-de-lucro-da-marmoraria)
- Consumer-side price ranges (2026): frontão R$60 to R$150 per metro corrido (SP). Cuba opening around R$182. Installed granite bancada R$350 to R$1,500/m². A 3 m straight bancada R$2.5k to R$7.5k — search summary of [Monitor do Mercado](https://monitordomercado.com.br/noticias/398543-quanto-custa-contratar-um-marmorista-para-instalar-bancada-de-cozinha-em-2026-e-por-que-o-recorte-da-pia-pesa-no-orcamento/) and [Lar Pontual](https://larpontual.com.br/portal/quanto-custa-bancada-de-granito) **[UNVERIFIED: content-farm style sources, ranges inconsistent]**
- Saia (an apron strip glued to the front edge so the top looks thicker) requires gluing and finishing work — same sources **[snippet-level]**
- Slab thickness (2 cm vs 3 cm) is a common cause of price differences between quotes, e.g. R$800 for the same bancada — [MPro Mármores](https://www.mpromarmoresegranitos.com.br/blog/marmoraria-na-zona-leste/)
- Cutouts close to edges, and undermount cubas or cooktops, leave less margin for error and take longer in the workshop — [Monitor do Mercado](https://monitordomercado.com.br/noticias/398543-quanto-custa-contratar-um-marmorista-para-instalar-bancada-de-cozinha-em-2026-e-por-que-o-recorte-da-pia-pesa-no-orcamento/) **[snippet-level]**
- Payment terms seen on marmoraria websites: 50% sinal to start production and 50% when installation is finished. Payment must be committed before production because the work is custom-made. PIX discounts for cash payment (one shop gives 11% off for cash, transfer or PIX). Cards from 2x or 3x up to 12x sem juros, or 2x sem juros on a payment link with card-company fees above that. Also "50% via PIX + 50% em até 3x" — search summary of [Fenix Marmoraria](https://wa.me/fenixmarmoraria?lang=bn_IN), [L&I Marmoraria](https://leimarmoraria.com/), [LC Mármores](https://www.lcmarmores.com/), [Marmoraria EcoGran](https://marmorariaecogran.com.br/) **[small sample; attributions per shop not individually verified]**
- CDC art. 40: the service provider must give a prior quote with the value of labour, materials and equipment, the payment terms, and start and finish dates. §1: valid for 10 days unless agreed otherwise (e.g. a stated 30 days prevails). §2: once approved it binds both parties and can only change by free negotiation. §3: the consumer does not pay third-party charges not in the quote — [LegJur CDC art. 40](https://www.legjur.com/legislacao/art/LEI_00080781990-40); [MPCE Decon](https://mpce.mp.br/decon/duvidas/servicos/cobranca-por-orcamento-e-legal/)
- Art. 39 VI forbids carrying out a service without a prior quote or the consumer's express authorisation — [TJDFT Direito Fácil](https://www.tjdft.jus.br/institucional/imprensa/campanhas-e-produtos/direito-facil/edicao-semanal/pratica-abusiva-servico-sem-orcamento)
- Charging for a quote is disputed. Idec says it should generally be free and that charging is abusive. Procon-GO allows charging if the consumer is told beforehand — [Idec](https://idec.org.br/consultas/dicas-e-direitos/orcamento-de-prestaco-de-servico-gratuito-ou-pago)
- Consumer guides advise checking that the quote itemises stone type and thickness, area, cutouts, edges, saia, frontão and installation, and confirms transport, hoisting (içamento) and joints (emendas) — search summary of consumer guides (Monitor do Mercado / Lar Pontual) **[snippet-level]**

### Inferences
- Pricing engine data model: material (R$/m², usable area, loss % per material or per job) + linear items (each edge profile, frontão/rodabanca, saia as linear or area) + unit items (cuba, cooktop, torneira hole, ralo, other cutouts) + services (installation per job, per m or per metro linear, freight or hoisting, a visit fee that is credited if the order closes) + payment conditions (sinal %, PIX discount %, number of instalments, card fee pass-through).
- Mandatory proposal fields to build in as defaults (CDC-driven): itemised materials and labour, payment terms, start and finish dates (or "X dias úteis após sinal + medição final"), validity (default 10 days, editable), who is responsible for the measurement, guarantee terms, and what is NOT included (hydraulic work, demolition, third-party costs).
- Showing thickness, edge type and the cutout list explicitly on the PDF fixes the "comparing apples to oranges" problem that clients run into between quotes.

### Gaps
- No authoritative source on typical warranty (garantia) terms used by marmorarias beyond the CDC legal minimum (90 days for durable goods and services under art. 26, from general knowledge, not fetched here) **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- No reliable survey of typical loss % (only the 20% rule of thumb and a 25% example).
- Rodabanca pricing specifically was not found. It is assumed to be priced per metro linear like frontão **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 3. Owner complaints about existing software

### Takeaway
Through search I found no first-hand owner complaints (YouTube comments, Facebook or Instagram posts, Play Store reviews). What exists is vendor copy, which by implication describes the pains: spreadsheets, paper, quotes lost in WhatsApp, slow quoting, and no signal on site (GranCut markets "100% offline"). The competitive landscape is crowded with monthly SaaS plans from about R$79 to R$300 or more, most of them web or desktop ERPs with 3D and cutting plans, which suggests the "complex and expensive" pain is real but unproven.

### Cited Findings
- Herogran: CRM, slab stock, cutting plan, service orders, production, finance. Runs in the browser on computer and phone. Advertised "a partir de R$250/mês", priced per active user (Piloto, Avançado and Executivo plans) — [Herogran](https://herograncrm.com.br/sistema-para-granito); [B2B Stack](https://www.b2bstack.com.br/product/herogran)
- Marmu: Básico R$199.90/month (unlimited quotes, 3 users). Intermediário R$299.90/month (10 users, NF-e). Free trial with no card. 3D quotes and PDF — [Marmu pricing](https://www.marmu.com.br/pricing/)
- PedraPRO: Básico R$199/month for 2 users — [PedraPRO](https://www.pedrapro.com.br/)
- iMarmore: R$99/month, or R$79/month paid annually, with 30 days free — [iMarmore](https://imarmore.com.br/)
- Marmoapp: Basic R$147/month, 7-day trial — [Marmoapp](https://www.marmoapp.com/)
- Enkad: R$183.75 by PIX or R$245 by card (billing period unclear) — [Enkad](https://enkad.com.br/software-para-marmoraria/)
- GranCut (Android): markets "100% offline after first access", with drafts autosaved and synced later, so you can draw, calculate and produce the PDF without signal — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.com.grancut.app); [GranCut](https://grancut.com.br/aplicativo-para-marmoraria/)
- Others found: Marmocalc, Marmo Smart, MarmoDraw (cutting plan and DXF for CNC), MarmoCut (cutting plan and CNC, sold with paid training), Stone Project (iOS), ERP Suite, Gransoft — [Marmocalc](https://marmocalc.com.br/), [Marmo Smart](https://www.marmosmart.com.br/), [MarmoDraw](https://www.wayssoft.com.br/marmodraw/), [MarmoCut Hotmart](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/marketplace/produtos/marmocut-software/O105724755O), [Stone Project](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stone-project-marmorarias/id1443840165?l=pt-BR), [ERP Suite](https://www.erpsuite.com.br/sistema-para-marmoraria), [Gransoft](https://www.gransoftweb.com/Tutorial/controle-e-gestao-de-estoques-para-marmoraria/55)
- A Workana job posting asks for an MVP of a web or PWA marmoraria quoting app with AI and OCR. This shows demand from at least one buyer for automated quoting from photos or drawings — [Workana](https://www.workana.com/job/desenvolvimento-de-mvp-marmoraria-app-web-pwa-para-orcamentos-automatizados-com-ia-e-ocr)
- An open-source offline PWA with an APK for marmoraria quotes exists on GitHub — [GitHub duducoBS](https://github.com/duducoBS/orcamentos-marmoraria)
- Vendor-stated pains: spreadsheets, quotes lost in WhatsApp, calculation errors on paper, hours per quote — [Marmocalc](https://marmocalc.com.br/) **[vendor marketing]**

### Inferences
- The market is not empty. There are 10+ marmoraria-specific products, many launched recently (several look like small or indie products). Differentiation has to come from the solo or tiny shop: phone-first, offline on site, a quote in a few minutes, a client approval link, and a low price or freemium, rather than 3D, CNC or ERP depth.
- Per-user pricing (Herogran) and 2-3 user caps suggest incumbents target shops with staff. A solo marmorista is underserved on price, though iMarmore at R$79-99 is already low.

### Gaps
- No primary owner complaints found (YouTube, Facebook groups, Instagram, Play Store reviews). The next step is to read Play Store reviews for GranCut, Marmoapp and the others directly, and to search marmorista Facebook groups manually.

## 4. End-client complaints about marmoraria quotes and services

### Takeaway
Reclame Aqui shows recurring themes: delays (sometimes months) after full or partial prepayment, measurement and cutting errors, wrong hole positions, missing accessories, delivered material that differs from what was quoted, poor communication and after-sales neglect, and disputes over who measured (architect vs marmoraria). These are mostly communication and record-keeping failures that software can address: written specs, measurement responsibility, deadlines, and status updates.

### Cited Findings
- "Serviço de marmoraria com atraso, corte errado e falta de comunicação". An interior professional said the quote looked attractive, but delays cost her days of idle installers' wages: "o orçamento é atrativo, mas os estresses e prejuízos seguintes fazem o valor final sair uma fortuna" — [Reclame Aqui: Mardil](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/mardil-marmores-e-granitos/servico-de-marmoraria-com-atraso-corte-errado-e-falta-de-comunicacao_yNYJrXOVr_pyIxOc/) (via search snippet)
- "Atraso na entrega do produto, erro no dimensionamento das peças e descaso com o Cliente". Material didn't match the quote. The shop replied that it followed the specifications the client chose — [Reclame Aqui: Margutti](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/marmoraria-margutti/atraso-na-entrega-do-produto-erro-no-dimensionamento-das-pecas-e-descaso-com-o-cliente_UCBqTKvgvKno_0mt/) (snippet)
- A shop disputed a measurement error: the cutting measurement came from the client's architect's project, and "quando a própria empresa mede, assume a responsabilidade" — search summary of Reclame Aqui responses **[snippet; exact company not pinned]**
- "Problemas na compra de bancadas: atraso, falta de acessório, furos incorretos e mau atendimento" — [Reclame Aqui: MV Marmoraria](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/mv-marmoraria/problemas-na-compra-de-bancadas-atraso-falta-de-acessorio-furos-incorretos-e-mau-atendimento_Kp4qm-HmIh0DHsKn/)

- Others: "Marmoraria descumpre prazo de entrega, não instala serviço pago e ignora cliente" ([MV](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/mv-marmoraria/marmoraria-descumpre-prazo-de-entrega-nao-instala-servico-pago-e-ignora-cliente_TCfuXlNYw6c2W3dN/)); "Atraso na instalação, pedra com avarias e falta de retorno" ([Staff](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/staff/atraso-na-instalacao-pedra-com-avarias-e-falta-de-retorno-sobre-solucao_krLIdJerT-gqXdTf/)); "Péssimo no atendimento pós-venda" ([MGR](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/mgr-marmoraria/pessimo-no-atendimento-pos-venda_F61S7Y-5mSOkFhMP/)); a refund request after a failed installation and cubas not returned ([Casa das Pedras](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/casa-das-pedras-ltda/cliente-solicita-cancelamento-e-estorno-apos-falha-na-confeccao-e-instalacao-de-marmores-e-nao-devolucao-de-cubas_WBxhL_H-R0c1Ljjv/))

### Inferences
- Product features that respond directly to these complaints: a client-approved spec sheet (stone, thickness, edges, cutout positions with a sketch or photo, client-supplied items such as the cuba), a "medição por: marmoraria / cliente / arquiteto" field with a liability note, a deadline with automatic status messages to the client (medido → em produção → pronto → instalação agendada), and records of client-owned items (cubas, cooktops) held at the shop.
- Hidden costs show up mostly as scope differences (thickness, installation not included, extra cutouts) rather than literal hidden fees. I found no Reclame Aqui titles that explicitly said "cobrança extra" in the sample **[limited sample]**.

## 5. WhatsApp Business, PIX links, digital approval in adjacent trades (patterns to borrow)

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the main sales and communication channel for about 8 in 10 Brazilian small businesses (Sebrae 2026). Apps for adjacent trades (serralheria, vidraçaria, marcenaria) have standardised a pattern: build the quote on the phone, send a branded PDF plus a web link by WhatsApp, the client approves or signs in the browser with no install (date and time recorded), and the quote moves through statuses (Aberto / Aprovado / Perdido). Offline use and digital approval are often reserved for the paid tier.

### Cited Findings
- Sebrae Pulso dos Pequenos Negócios (12th edition, 8.2k respondents, Feb-Mar 2026): 82% of MEIs and MPEs name WhatsApp as their main communication and sales channel, up 1 percentage point on 2024. 73% sell through digital channels, stable since 2021 — [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/dados/whatsapp-se-consolida-nas-vendas-on-line-enquanto-facebook-e-lojas-proprias-perdem-folego/)
- Sebrae: WhatsApp is the main communication channel for 80% of service businesses — [Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/cultura-empreendedora/whatsapp-e-o-principal-meio-de-comunicacao-para-80-dos-negocios-de-servico/)
- PIX is the main payment method of MEI clients (51%) in a state survey (Sebrae MT, Apr 2025). No national figure was found — [Sebrae MT Pulsos 2025](https://datasebrae.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Pulsos-2025-1a-Edicao-MT.pdf)
- Interaup (separate apps for serralheiro, marceneiro and vidraceiro): branded PDF, the client opens a browser link to approve with no install, signature recorded with date and time — [Interaup serralheiro](https://interaup.com.br/app-para-serralheiro/); [vidraceiro](https://interaup.com.br/app-para-vidraceiro/); [marceneiro](https://interaup.com.br/app-para-marceneiro/)
- Vidroo (vidraçaria): R$69.90/month paid plan includes digital approval, which the free plan lacks. Quote statuses Aberto / Aprovado / Perdido — [Vidroo](https://vidroo.com.br/)
- Fechou: phone quote, PDF sent "antes do concorrente", works without internet, signature stamped on the PDF — [Fechou](https://fechou.pro/)
- OrçaPro: one-click approval via the link — [OrçaPro](https://orcamento.org/)
- Fazer Orçamento (Play Store): templates for marcenaria and serralheria, digital signature, PDF or link — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.com.fazerorcamento&listing=site_fo&hl=en_US)
- Meu Serralheiro Pro: branded PDF for WhatsApp. The basic plan is capped at 30 quotes per month — [Meu Serralheiro Pro](https://meuserralheiropro.com/)
- A guide for workshop quotes on WhatsApp recommends separate line items, clear values, written deadline and validity, and recording the approval with date and time — [UAI](https://www.uai.com.br/?p=187657)
- Furniture cutting-plan and quote app: CorteCloud (free on the App Store) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1489807401)

### Inferences
- Patterns worth borrowing: a branded PDF plus a live link (the client can see the latest version), one-tap approval with name, timestamp and IP recorded, option or variant selection by the client (e.g. granito A vs quartzo B, with or without saia), a PIX key or QR code (or payment link) for the sinal right on the approval page, quote status tracking with follow-up reminders, and offline creation.
- The orders, production and CRM layer after approval is where trade-specific quote apps are thinner. The marmoraria ERPs are heavy there. A lightweight middle (approval → order → simple production board → installation date → client notifications) is the likely gap.
- I did not search for or verify "Orçamentor", "Marcenaria Fácil" or Promob specifically. Promob is known (from general knowledge) as a desktop 3D design tool for furniture, not a quoting-approval app **[UNVERIFIED]**.

### Gaps
- No data on WhatsApp Business catalog use by marmorarias, or on PIX-link adoption in the trade specifically.
- No legal analysis of the validity of click-approval under Brazilian law (MP 2.200-2/2001 and Lei 14.063/2020 allow simple electronic signatures. This comes from general knowledge and was not verified here) **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 6. Market size: number of marmorarias in Brazil

### Takeaway
The official-style estimates are old: about 7,000 marmorarias and 50k+ workers (2008-era government and Fundacentro figures). Current CNPJ-based counts for CNAE 2391-5/03 ("Aparelhamento de placas e execução de trabalhos em mármore, granito, ardósia e outras pedras") put about 19k to 24k active companies in 2026. Most are micro or small firms. Many solo marmoristas probably operate as MEI or informally under other CNAEs, so the real number of quoting entities may be higher **[inference]**.

### Inferences
- A reasonable addressable base is about 19k to 24k formal marmorarias (CNAE-registered), most of them micro. At iMarmore-to-Marmu pricing (R$79 to R$300 per month), the theoretical formal-market revenue ceiling is very roughly R$18M to R$86M per year at 100% penetration. That is a niche, which argues for expanding to adjacent trades (vidraçaria, serralheria, marcenaria) or upselling payments.
- The gap between the 7k (2008) and 19-24k (2026) figures probably reflects formalisation (MEI/Simples) and counting method (CNPJs vs establishments), not necessarily tripled activity **[inference]**.

### Gaps
- I found no current Abirochas or Sebrae national count of marmorarias. The Abirochas annual "Balanço" covers exports, not the number of shops.
- No breakdown by MEI vs ME vs EPP, or by employee count, for CNAE 2391-5/03. That would need Receita open data or IBGE CEMPRE.
